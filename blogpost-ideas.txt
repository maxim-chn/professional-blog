2. Agent as a partner
  - What are the suitable fields?
    A: where human partner is a professional.
  - What are the types of responsibilities?
    A: 1. human is a professional, offloading workload. Partnership is in freedom of addition/transformation of
          small workload
       2. human is a novice. Partnership is in agent delivering small steps of execution that teach human and
          perspective supply.
  - How to deal with trust issues?
    A: prepare to fail. This is essential for mutual learn path.
  - Where to start?
    A: a coffee conversation. Agent is to explain domain through simple answers, suitable for a chat over a coffee.
  - How to introduce Agent to a project? I.e., through commands/skills.
  - How to maintain project with Agent? I.e., after considerable testing - use navigation of skills. It helps to iron out
    how exactly Agent understands available capabilities.
