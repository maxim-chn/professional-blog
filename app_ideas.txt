  What is it? A personal manager that helps with budget allocation and tracking.
    Capability that involve analysis and decision making, such as planning and insights generation, are out of scope.
  What are core capabilities?
    1. Overview.
      It is an infographic of monthly status that communicates the:
        - break down of allocated budget into consumed and remaining.
        - remaining budget
        - overspent
    2. Allocate.
      It is graphically accessible mechanism to manually allocate a monthly budget.
      Important traits and capabilities:
        - category, such as groceries, health, vehicle, presents etc.
        - limit
        - grace buffer.
          An allocation beyond expected limit for special cases. For example, christmas presents purchase usually 
          tends to "spill over".
        - recurring items.
          This is an option, used by default, that "spends" recurring items automatically.
    3. Recurring items.
      These are items that belong to a certain category and are expected to occur monthly.
    4. Re-allocate.
      Automatically allocates last month budget to the current month.
    5. Spend
      It is a graphically accessible mechanism to register spent sum to a particular category.
      We subract the value from limit, making sure that limit cannot be negative when presented.
      I.e. there is a need to track limit as negative, however display value for any negative value is 0.
    6. Spend reversal
      A graphically accessible mechism that de-registers a spent sum.
